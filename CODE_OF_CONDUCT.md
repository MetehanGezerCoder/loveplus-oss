# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Love+ to be a welcoming, respectful, and constructive open-source project. Contributors and maintainers are expected to treat one another with professionalism and empathy regardless of background, identity, experience level, or point of view.

## Expected behavior

Examples of positive behavior include:

- giving actionable, respectful feedback;
- assuming good intent while still being clear about technical or security concerns;
- keeping discussions focused on the work;
- respecting privacy, especially because this project handles sensitive relationship and location data;
- acknowledging mistakes and correcting them quickly.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized or insulting language, doxxing, publishing private information, or intentionally weakening security/privacy protections.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove, reject, or lock contributions and discussions that violate this policy. Serious or repeated violations may result in temporary or permanent exclusion from project spaces.

For conduct concerns that should not be discussed publicly, contact the maintainer privately through the contact method listed on the maintainer's GitHub profile.
